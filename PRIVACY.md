# MindReader privacy

MindReader sends the category you select and the questions and answers in your current game to Anna's AI service to generate the next question or final guess. It never asks you to type the secret answer. Your confirmation of a final guess is handled locally.

The app keeps the current game in memory only. It does not save game history, use Anna Storage, collect analytics, use advertising trackers, or contact an external service directly. Reloading or closing the app clears its local game state. Anna and its AI providers process requests under their own policies and normal quota rules.

No extra API key is required. MindReader only requests Anna's LLM completion and window lifecycle capabilities. Questions and guesses may be inaccurate; the experience is an entertainment game.
